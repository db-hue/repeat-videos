Shadowing with a loop, step by step

  1. Pick any native-speed video — an interview, a vlog, a news clip — and paste its URL into repeat-videos.com.
  2. Drag the A and B handles around one sentence or phrase (2–6 seconds works best).
  3. Set the speed to 0.75× if the passage is fast. Pitch stays natural — it just gives your ears more time.
  4. Listen twice, then speak along with the loop. Imitate the rhythm and intonation, not just the words.
  5. Move the speed back to 1× and keep shadowing until you stay with the speaker. Then slide the window to the next sentence.

Why a looper instead of scrubbing YouTube back

  • The segment repeats hands-free — you shadow continuously instead of hunting for the timestamp after every attempt.
  • Precise windows — the A-B handles land on tenths of a second, so the loop starts exactly on the first syllable.
  • Saved per video — loop points and speed are remembered, so a dialogue you drill this week is set up the moment you reopen it.
  • A play counter — 20 repetitions of one sentence is a real shadowing set; the counter shows you did them.
  • Shareable drills — tutors can send students a link that opens the exact sentence at the right speed.
  • No distractions — no recommended-videos sidebar, no comments, no autoplay jumping to an unrelated video when a long episode or playlist ends. Just your material, repeating.

What to loop at each level

Beginner

Slow, clearly spoken learner content. Loop full sentences at 1× (the speech is already slow) and focus on copying intonation.

Intermediate

Native vlogs and interviews. Loop 3–5 second chunks at 0.75×, then repeat the same chunk at 1× before moving on.

Advanced

Fast conversational or regional-accent material. Loop the passages you didn't catch, at 1× — the goal is parsing at full speed, with 0.75× only to untangle the hardest seconds.

FAQ

What is shadowing?

Speaking along with native audio, imitating pronunciation, rhythm, and intonation. Looping one sentence makes it repeatable enough to actually train.

How do I repeat one sentence of a video?

Drag the A and B handles around it — playback repeats exactly that window until you move on.

Does slowed speech sound unnatural?

At 0.75× it stays close to natural and much easier to parse; pitch doesn't change. 0.5× is there for the fastest passages.
