Drill choreography section by section

  1. Paste the tutorial's YouTube URL into repeat-videos.com — it loops automatically.
  2. Drag the A and B handles around the move or eight-count you're learning. Only that part repeats.
  3. Slow it to 0.5× or 0.75× to see the footwork clearly — the music keeps its pitch, just slower.
  4. Mark the counts slowly until the sequence is in your body, then step the speed back up to full tempo.
  5. Slide the loop window to the next section and connect them.

Speed laddering for fast choreography

For a fast section: loop it at 0.5× and mark it until the order of moves is automatic. Move to 0.75× and add full energy. Then 1× — and if performance nerves are a factor, a few runs at 1.25× make full tempo feel easy.

FAQ

How do I loop part of a tutorial?

Drag the A and B handles around the section — one move or one eight-count — and it repeats hands-free.

Does slowed music sound wrong?

No — pitch is preserved at every speed from 0.5× to 2×, so it just sounds slower, not deeper.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, right in the mobile browser — no app install.
